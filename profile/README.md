# The Next Chapter

*A space to keep building, learning, and connecting.*

The Next Chapter is an independent community of engineers whose paths crossed at Microsoft ADC.

As we move into new opportunities and experiences, this space gives us a place to stay connected, share knowledge, collaborate on projects, and explore ideas together.

## What We're Exploring

- **Building together** — Side projects, experiments, and open-source contributions.
- **Sharing knowledge** — Technical resources, tools, ideas, and lessons learned.
- **Discovering opportunities** — Sharing relevant roles, referrals, and collaborations.
- **Staying connected** — Learning from one another and supporting each other's growth.

## Get Involved

This is a space for members to share ideas, explore projects, and contribute where they can.

Whether you have something to build, something to share, or simply an interest in what others are working on, you're welcome to participate.

## Community Principles

- **Respect people.** Treat others with respect and engage constructively, even when opinions differ.
- **Share knowledge.** Exchange ideas, give credit where it's due, and help others grow.
- **Build responsibly.** Contribute only work and materials you're authorized to share.
- **Respect confidentiality.** Don't disclose confidential information, proprietary materials, or information you're not authorized to share.
- **Protect privacy.** Respect the privacy of community members and others.
- **Take initiative.** Help make this a space where people can learn, collaborate, and create opportunities.

## What's Next?

We're taking things one step at a time, seeing what we can build together, and making room for new possibilities.

**Different paths. Shared roots. New beginnings.**
